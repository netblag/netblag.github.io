The Path

How I turn learning into projects.

02
  1. 01 — STAGE ONE

    Understand

    I start by understanding the problem itself: what needs to be solved, what information is available, what is missing, and what success would actually mean. Then I break the idea into smaller, testable parts before choosing the implementation.

    Problem solvingData preparationStatistics
  2. 02 — STAGE TWO

    Experiment

    Once the problem is clear, I experiment. That can mean exploratory analysis, feature engineering, training a model, comparing approaches, or testing whether an idea works. I care about understanding the result, not only chasing a metric.

    PythonMachine LearningModel evaluation
  3. 03 — STAGE THREE

    Build

    The last step is turning the result into software: an Android application, an API, a dashboard, or a small tool. I’m interested in the point where an idea leaves the notebook and becomes something that can actually be used.

    KotlinAndroidAPIs
