The Path

How I turn learning into projects.

02
  1. 01 — STAGE ONE

    Understand

    I start by understanding the problem itself: what needs to be solved, what information is available, what is missing, and what success would actually mean. I break the idea into smaller, testable parts before choosing the implementation.

    Data preparationStatisticsExploration
  2. 02 — STAGE TWO

    Experiment

    Once the problem is clear, I experiment. That can mean exploratory analysis, feature engineering, training a model, comparing approaches, or testing whether an idea works. I care about understanding the result, not only chasing a metric.

    Machine LearningFeature engineeringModel evaluation
  3. 03 — STAGE THREE

    Build

    The last step is turning the result into software: an Android application, an API, a dashboard, or a small tool. I’m interested in the point where an idea leaves the notebook and becomes something that can actually be used.

    SoftwareAndroidPractical applications

building.

I’m a software engineering student developing toward data science and machine learning while continuing to build real software. I don’t see these as separate paths: understanding a problem, working with data, writing code, and building something usable are all parts of the same process.

  • Understand firstA clear problem is worth more than an impressive model.
  • Experiment, then explainIf I can’t say why something works, I keep digging.
